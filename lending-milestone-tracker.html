Where the funnel breaks

Document upload (income verification) is the single largest drop-off. Borrowers who hit the upload step and idle for 5+ minutes abandon at high rates.
Wait time during credit assessment causes a second wave of attrition. Borrowers find alternatives or lose momentum over 1–4 business days.
Manual underwriting costs $150–300 per application regardless of outcome. At 22% STP, ~78% of volume carries full manual cost.

Roadmap opportunities

CDR consent flow: replace document upload with live bank data pull. Eliminates the primary abandonment bottleneck at Stage 3.
AI decisioning engine: auto-approve/decline standard applications in <30 min. Eliminates the 1–2 day analyst queue at Stage 5.
Streamlined settlement: reduce drawdown lag from 3–5 days to 1–2 days. Captures conversion lost in the final mile.
Benchmark sources: 68% online financial services application abandonment rate (ResolvePay, 2024); document upload confirmed as primary abandonment trigger; idle time on upload step is the key behavioural signal (Kommunicate, Lightico, Plaid 2024–25); leading digital lenders achieve 80–90% STP vs ~22% for traditional banks (Pennant Technologies 2025); manual underwriting $150–$300 per application vs $5–$15 for STP applications (Pennant Technologies 2025); personal loan approval rate ~65% of submitted applications (Pennant / NextGen 2025); CDR adoption in Australia confirmed as low; government simplifying consent rules in 2026 to improve uptake (ACCC, CDR.gov.au, 2025).
